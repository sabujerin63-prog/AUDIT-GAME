1st Year M.Com Study Notes: Module 4

Quick Reference Guide for Audit Sampling & Analytical Procedures

1. Meaning of Audit Sampling (SA 530)

Audit sampling means testing less than 100% of items in a population to form an overall conclusion about that entire population.

2. Main Sampling Methods

• Random Selection: Every item has an equal chance of being picked (using random numbers).
• Stratified Selection: Dividing a population into similar subgroups (e.g., checking big values separately).
• Systematic Selection: Picking every nth item (e.g. Every 10th invoice) using a fixed interval.
• Haphazard Selection: Picking items arbitrarily without structured math, but avoiding personal bias.

3. Factors Influencing Sample Size

• Lower Tolerable Error: Requires a LARGER sample size.
• Higher Expected Error: Requires a LARGER sample size.
• Strong Internal Controls: Auditor can use a SMALLER sample size.
• Large Population Size: Has almost NO effect on sample size!

4. Test Checking

Examining representative sample vouchers to save audit time. Must include Cut-Off checks (matching correct financial year dates) and verifying manager signatures.

5. Analytical Procedures (SA 520)

Studying plausible relationships between financial and non-financial figures (Trend Analysis, Ratio Analysis, Reasonableness tests). Verbal excuses from management must always be verified with independent documentary evidence.
